Method & data limits

The screening index is our transparent calculation from separate Places category counts: demand and supporting categories increase it; direct competition reduces it. Google does not produce this index or a GO decision. It is a commercial-activity proxy, not measured foot traffic, visits, rent, frontage, or sales.
